# PrepMate AI

### AI-Powered Personalized Placement Preparation Assistant

PrepMate AI is a multi-agent AI-powered placement preparation platform designed to help students understand their career direction, identify skill gaps, improve their resumes, prepare for interviews, and track their placement readiness through a personalized workflow.

Instead of providing the same recommendations to every student, PrepMate AI analyzes the student's profile, resume, skills, education, experience, projects, certifications, and career interests to generate personalized recommendations.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

Students often struggle with questions such as:

- Which career domain is suitable for me?
- Which job roles match my current profile?
- What skills am I missing?
- What should I learn next?
- Is my resume suitable for my target role?
- How should I prepare for interviews?
- How ready am I for placements?

PrepMate AI brings these activities together into a single intelligent platform.
